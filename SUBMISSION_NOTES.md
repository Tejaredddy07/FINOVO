# FINOVO — Submission Notes

## Problem statement
College students often struggle to track expenses, stay within budgets, save consistently, and understand whether a purchase fits their finances.

## Proposed solution
FINOVO is a student-focused personal finance management interface combining expense tracking, budgeting, savings goals, financial insights, and reports.

## Features demonstrated
1. Dashboard with balance, income, expenses, savings, spending chart, and Financial Health Score.
2. Add Expense modal.
3. Transaction list.
4. Budget category progress.
5. Savings goal cards.
6. Monthly report summary.
7. Quick Help AI for application guidance.
8. AI Financial Assistant with sample affordability and savings answers.
9. Responsive dark UI with Space Grotesk and DM Sans fonts.

## Technology
HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, Chart.js CDN, Google Fonts.

## Limitations
The assistants use demo rule-based responses and sample data; they are not live AI integrations. This prototype has no production backend, real authentication, or persistent database. Those can be implemented in the full version.
